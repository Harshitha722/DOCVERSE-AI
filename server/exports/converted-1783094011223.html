DECISIONMESH AI: Hackathon Presentation Guide

This guide provides exactly what you need to record to satisfy the Deliverables section of your Hackathon PDF. The PDF requires two distinct 5-minute videos. You can record them separately or back-to-back for a ~9-minute combined video.

Part 1: The 5-Minute Demo Video (Business Use Case)

Goal: Prove your platform meets the 7 end-to-end workflow requirements from the PDF.

0:00 - 0:30 | Introduction & Domain

What to show: The frontend dashboard home screen. What to say:

"Hello judges! For this challenge, we built DECISIONMESH AI, a reusable Agentic Decision Intelligence Platform. We chose the B2B SaaS Sales domain. Our business challenge is closing enterprise software deals, where the decision points are complex and require tracking buyer objections, ROI needs, and compliance blockers."

0:30 - 1:30 | Requirement 1 & 2: Ingest & Gather Context

What to show: Uploading test_sales_transcript.txt into the UI. What to say:

"Our platform is not a simple chatbot. It is a planner-driven architecture. To demonstrate, I am uploading a raw discovery call transcript where a customer expresses price and security objections. Under the hood, our system chunks this document and embeds it using Google Generative AI, storing it in a Qdrant Vector Database to gather context."

1:30 - 3:00 | Requirement 3, 4, & 5: Analyze, Recommend, Explain

What to show: Click "Analyze" and let the Decision Report load. Hover over the High/Medium/Low tiers. What to say:

"Our dynamic orchestrator detects the sales domain and spins up specialized Pydantic-AI agents that run in parallel. Here is the output. Instead of a chat response, the agents analyze the business context and generate three prioritized scenarios: Close-ready, Objection recovery, and No-decision prevention. For the 'High' priority recommendation, it explicitly tells the user the next best action, the expected business outcome, and provides explainable reasoning by linking directly back to the evidence in the source transcript."

3:00 - 5:00 | Requirement 6 & 7: Human-in-the-Loop & Memory Learning

What to show: Scroll to the "Customer Outcome" buttons (Won/Lost). Click "Lost" and type "Needs stronger ROI". What to say:

"Crucially, our system supports Human-in-the-Loop. Before an action is blindly executed, the human reviews the confidence scores. Furthermore, we built a Shared Memory architecture. If this deal is lost because the customer needed better ROI proof, I can record that outcome here. This feedback is semantically embedded back into Qdrant. The next time our planner agents orchestrate a similar scenario, they will retrieve this memory and proactively recommend stronger ROI proof up-front. That completes the 7-step workflow requirement."

Part 2: The 4 to 5-Minute Architecture Walkthrough

Goal: Satisfy the 70% Platform Evaluation Criteria by proving reusability, extensibility, and quality.

0:00 - 1:30 | The Tech Stack & "Not just RAG"

What to show: Open app/main.py and pyproject.toml in VS Code to show FastAPI and dependencies. What to say:

"Moving to the architecture, we built a fully decoupled Next.js frontend and a FastAPI backend. To prove this isn't just a standard RAG app, we used Pydantic-AI and Google Gemini 1.5 Flash. Standard RAG returns unstructured text. We use Pydantic schemas to strictly enforce that the AI agents return structured JSON, ensuring our UI gets reliable, predictable data arrays."

1:30 - 3:00 | Dynamic Orchestration & Extensibility

What to show: Open app/agents/factory.py or app/core/orchestrator.py. What to say:

"Here is our Agent Factory. This proves the platform's reusability. The orchestrator dynamically reads YAML domain ontologies. If we wanted to pivot this platform from SaaS Sales to Healthcare or Staffing tomorrow, we don't have to rewrite the code. We simply drop in a new YAML file. The factory will read it and spin up entirely new agents tailored to that new domain."

3:00 - 4:30 | Shared Memory Design

What to show: Open app/memory/store.py and show the Qdrant connection. What to say:

"For memory, we use a hybrid approach. We run Qdrant as our vector store. But we don't just store documents—we store Customer Outcomes. By separating the document RAG from the Memory RAG, our LLM agents get two distinct context windows injected in parallel: one for the immediate facts, and one for historical enterprise wisdom."

4:30 - 5:00 | Conclusion

What to show: Your face / wide shot of the UI. What to say:

"By combining parallel Pydantic-AI agents, strict JSON guardrails, and a Qdrant-backed continuous learning loop, DECISIONMESH AI is a truly extensible Agentic Decision Intelligence Platform. Thank you!"
